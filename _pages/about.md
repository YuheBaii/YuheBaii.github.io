Hi, I'm Yuhe Bai, a Master's student at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://www.fu-berlin.de/en/). My work focuses on **diffusion models** and their applications in **AI for Science**, where I'm interested in how generative models can help us understand and design complex scientific systems.

On this site I share my projects and write technical notes on generative modeling, machine learning, and the papers I'm reading.

Research Interests
======
- Diffusion and score-based generative models
- AI for Science
- Generative modeling for scientific data
